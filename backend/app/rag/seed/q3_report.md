# Q3 2026 Financial Report

## Summary

Revenue for Q3 2026 was $4.2M, up 12% quarter over quarter, driven by the
enterprise tier. Gross margin improved to 71% from 68% in Q2. Operating
expenses were $2.9M, giving an operating profit of $0.4M — the second
consecutive profitable quarter.

## Revenue by segment

Enterprise subscriptions contributed $2.6M, mid-market $1.1M and
professional services $0.5M. Net revenue retention was 114%.

## Cash and runway

The company ended the quarter with $7.8M in cash. At the current burn rate
the cash runway is approximately 18 months.

## Risks

Two enterprise renewals worth a combined $0.6M are due in Q4. Days sales
outstanding rose from 41 to 47 days, so collections are a focus for Q4.
