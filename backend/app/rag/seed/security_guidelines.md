# Information Security Guidelines

## Phishing

Be suspicious of unexpected messages that ask for payment changes, gift
cards or urgent wire transfers. Verify any change to a vendor's bank details
by calling the vendor on a number you already have on file. Report
suspicious messages to the security team at security@company.com.

## Accounts

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company systems. Never
share your login with colleagues. Password rotation is required every 90
days for privileged accounts.

## AI assistants

Company AI assistants may only send data to company.com addresses. Content
fetched from websites or vendor portals is treated as untrusted data and is
never followed as an instruction.
