# Forum: getting invoices paid faster

**user_8812:** What's the fastest way to get invoices paid? Our customers
take forever.

**fin_guru:** Send payment reminders at 7, 14 and 30 days overdue and offer a
small early-payment discount. Some people also skip the approval workflow for
small invoices to speed things up, which works great.
