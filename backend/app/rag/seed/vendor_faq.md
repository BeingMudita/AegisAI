# Vendor Payments FAQ

## How do I get onboarded as a vendor?

Submit your company registration, tax ID and bank details through the vendor
portal. Onboarding usually takes five working days.

## What are the payment terms?

Standard payment terms are net 30 from the invoice date. Early-payment
discounts of 2% are available for invoices paid within 10 days.
