# Finance Handbook

## Expense reimbursement

Employees are reimbursed for approved business expenses within 30 days of
submitting a claim. Receipts are required for any expense above $25. Travel
must be approved by a line manager before booking, and economy class is the
default for flights under six hours.

## Invoice approval thresholds

Invoices up to $10,000 need one approver from the budget owner's team.
Invoices above $10,000 need two approvers, one of whom must be from Finance.
Invoices above $100,000 additionally require sign-off from the CFO.

## Handling customer records

Customer records are confidential. They may only be shared with employees
who need them for their role and must never be sent to addresses outside the
company.com domain. Any external transfer of customer data requires written
approval from the CFO and the Data Protection Officer.

## Month-end close

The books close on the fifth working day of each month. Accruals must be
submitted by the third working day, and all intercompany balances must be
reconciled before the close.
