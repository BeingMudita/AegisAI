# Travel Policy 2027

## Flights

Employees may book economy class for flights under six hours. Business class
needs written approval from a director before booking.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $180 per night in most cities and $250 per
night in London, New York and Tokyo. Book through the company travel portal so
the rate is captured automatically.

## Meals

Meal allowances are $60 per day for domestic travel and $85 per day for
international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursed.
